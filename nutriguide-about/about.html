About NutriGuide

Meet the team who built this site.

NutriGuide was created to help patients understand, in plain language, how nutrition and diet affect their health — and what they can actually do about it. Every claim on this site is tied to a real, cited source, so patients and their care teams can look deeper whenever they want.
